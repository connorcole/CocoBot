1. Who runs CocoBot and what this policy covers

CocoBot is an independent Discord application. This Privacy Policy explains what information CocoBot processes through its calendar, tickets, voice-room management, moderation, tasks, gaming groups, polls, roles, analytics, leveling, AI/FAQ, backups, and related commands, and what information may be processed when you visit its GitHub Pages legal pages. It does not replace Discord's Privacy Policy.

Hosting model matters. CocoBot v2 is presently distributed as a self-hosted beta: its operating database is on the computer or server that runs a particular bot instance. The person or organization that operates that instance controls the files, backups, and operational access and must handle data requests for that instance. The software publisher cannot necessarily access, correct, or delete data on another person's independently hosted copy. If CocoBot becomes centrally hosted, this Policy must be revised to identify the actual host, operator/controller roles, vendors, and geographic processing locations.

Server administrators may configure optional features and determine which channels and roles those features affect. That does not remove the legal operator's obligations or override a member's applicable rights.

The data actually processed depends on the enabled modules and how you use CocoBot:

The bot does not record voice audio. Routine message-count statistics and leveling do not require storing the text of messages. The AI question is not normally saved to the bot's SQLite database, but Discord and the configured AI provider may process and retain the interaction in accordance with their own policies. The bot does not require real names, postal addresses, credit-card data, health records, or passwords to provide these functions. Please avoid submitting sensitive information in any event, ticket, task, poll, or AI prompt.

Not all polls are anonymous: v2 saves the Discord user ID attached to votes in order to enforce one vote per member and permit vote changes.

3. How and why data is used

Data is used to respond to authorized commands, maintain member and server settings, display events and tasks, deliver reminders, manage support tickets, execute staff moderation requests, protect against limited forms of spam, administer roles, provide requested reports and participation features, support backups, investigate incidents, and comply with lawful obligations and Discord's requirements.

CocoBot does not sell, license, or monetize Discord API data; share it with advertising networks or data brokers; send unsolicited marketing messages to members; or use message content obtained from Discord to train machine-learning models. It does not make automated eligibility decisions about housing, employment, insurance, or credit.

Where a privacy law requires a stated lawful basis (for example, some GDPR-covered processing), that basis depends on the particular operator, server configuration, and context. Common candidate bases include fulfilling a requested service, legitimate interests in limited security and operation where properly assessed, and legal compliance. A server administrator enabling analytics or AI is not automatically the member's individual consent. Where actual consent, contractual safeguards, an opt-out, or additional notices are legally required, those must be provided before the related processing begins.

4. When conversation text can be accessed

Most CocoBot commands operate on messages, events, or identifiers supplied through slash commands and buttons rather than silently archiving entire discussions. Two features may retrieve existing conversation text: (1) a staff member's request for a ticket transcript of up to approximately 250 messages in that ticket channel; and (2) an authorized staff member's request for an AI channel summary of up to 50 recent accessible messages, when the bot has the necessary Discord Message Content Intent and an AI key. Optional anti-spam watches message timing and deletes bursts; current v2 does not perform continuous AI analysis of all message text.

Transcript files are generated on demand, delivered to authorized staff via Discord, and may be downloaded or retained by recipients. Closing a ticket does not delete its Discord channel. A private or ephemeral bot response does not prevent Discord or other relevant providers from processing the data transmitted through it.

5. Who can see data and when it is disclosed

Depending on access and configuration, information may be visible to:

  • Other server members: calendars, tasks, suggestions, polls, event attendee lists, announcements, and other content shared in public channels.
  • Server moderators or administrators: warnings, support tickets, participation summaries, server settings, exports, and member-specific records as allowed by their permissions. Admins may download a server-wide JSON backup containing IDs, tickets, warnings and other records.
  • Discord: interactions and posts within the Discord platform are handled by Discord under its own policies.
  • OpenAI API (optional): when generative AI is enabled, the question and relevant FAQ context, or requested channel messages for summarization, are transmitted to the configured OpenAI API. The default API policy states these inputs are not used to train models unless an account opts in, and abuse monitoring information may be retained for up to 30 days under default settings, with exceptions. Check the provider's current API data controls.
  • Hosting and technical service providers: only those actually engaged by the person operating the instance; access and geographic location depend on that deployment. The public legal pages are hosted using GitHub Pages (see Section 10).
  • Authorities or others where required: for valid legal requirements, security protection, or a user-directed disclosure permitted by applicable law and Discord policies.

CocoBot is not integrated with marketing trackers, advertising exchanges, or data brokers.

6. Where data is stored and secured

The current beta stores server-related records in a local SQLite database (typically data/cocobot.sqlite3, including associated SQLite WAL files) on the host running the application. The bot token and any optional AI credential are configured through an environment file or variables, not the main SQLite data tables. The host may create copies through backups and downloaded transcripts; authorized Discord administrators may receive an exported backup file.

Current beta limitation: SQLite is not encrypted at rest at the database/application layer by default. An independently configured encrypted disk and encrypted backups may protect storage, but the code does not enforce either safeguard. Database files and backup exports should not be publicly accessible and require appropriate host encryption, access control, secret management, and deletion protections before broad deployment. We cannot guarantee absolute security. Discord's current Developer Terms require encryption at rest and reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, and the software/host must satisfy those operationally.

7. Retention and deletion

The v2 beta has no general automatic retention schedule. Individual calendars, tickets, warnings, tasks, participation records, voting data, and settings can remain in the host's database until deleted manually, changed by a moderator, or removed during a data-maintenance operation. Some Discord messages and downloaded exports can exist separately, even if database rows are removed. Ticket closure does not itself erase ticket messages. Reminder-delivery records are stored so the bot does not repeat notifications.

This is a known prelaunch deficiency, not a declaration that indefinite retention is appropriate. The operator must implement and follow a documented retention schedule that deletes data when no longer needed for the legitimate bot function, promptly responds to valid user/platform deletion requests, handles server removal, and addresses backups, subject only to narrow legal exceptions. A policy alone cannot make the current database compliant. Specific retention periods will be published after the operator has actually implemented and tested them.

8. Requests to access, correct, export, or delete data

To submit a privacy request, contact connorcole7@gmail.com and describe the affected Discord server ID and user ID. We may ask you to verify control of the applicable Discord account by a safe in-app method. Never provide a password, one-time code, bot token, or API key. We will evaluate verified requests and respond according to applicable law and Discord's developer requirements.

Current limitation: CocoBot v2 does not yet provide a complete self-service /coco privacy delete or automatic delete-on-uninstall command. Requests for data in the operator's own instance must be handled through a documented manual process until those controls exist; requests involving a separately operated self-hosted instance must be addressed to that instance's operator. Deletion cannot necessarily retract content independently held by Discord, staff who downloaded copies, or service providers subject to lawful retention.

You may also have rights to a copy of your data, to correct inaccuracies, to object to certain processing, to withdraw consent where processing is actually based on consent, or to complain to a regulator, depending on the laws applicable to your circumstances. No statement in this Policy diminishes non-waivable rights.

9. Children and sensitive data

CocoBot is not directed to children younger than Discord's country-specific minimum age (at least 13). Server owners should not configure it to solicit data from such children. We do not intentionally request passwords, payment details, financial account information, medical records, biometric data, or other sensitive information; members should not add those to tickets or AI prompts. If we learn the service has improperly obtained protected information, the operator will investigate and delete it or take other required steps.
